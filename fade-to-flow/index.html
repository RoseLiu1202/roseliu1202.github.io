Fade to Flow

2024
Digital Film + Sound Composition
Duration: 4 minutes 58 seconds

Artist Statement

Fade to Flow is a sound and moving image work that reflects on identity and the transient nature of the self. The body is continually reshaped through aging and metabolism, while emotions, thoughts, and beliefs shift in constant flux. In dialogue with Descartes’ process of doubt, I meditated on what kind of “thinking thing” I am, only to realize that the sense of a singular, enduring “I” is itself an illusion. Inspired by the Buddhist concept of anatta (non-self), the work embraces the idea that all things exist in ceaseless transformation, interwoven by causality and karmic connections.

By presenting the self as a fluid cognitive construct, Fade to Flow dismantles the illusion of permanence. Blending scientific and philosophical perspectives, it reveals the dissonance between consciousness and the body, opening space for a fragile yet flowing sense of existence.
