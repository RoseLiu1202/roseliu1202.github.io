涅槃 (Nirvana)

2024
Digital Film + Site-specific Performance
Duration: 4 minutes 58 seconds

Artist Statement

Nirvana is a meditation on destruction and rebirth, realized as a site-specific performance grounded in the body’s encounter with a burned landscape. Through philosophical reflection and embodied experience, the work explores how, after profound loss or psychological wounding, the human spirit rekindles vitality through reconstruction and transformation. The piece began when I stood before a scorched coastline along the Pacific—black soil, salt air, and faint green sprouts shimmering through ash. In that moment, meaning arose not from thought but from sensation; my body resonated with the land’s quiet persistence. The burned earth mirrored an unspoken inner landscape—a subconscious terrain marked by pain yet pulsing with the will to live.

The work draws on Maurice Merleau-Ponty’s concept of the flesh of the world, which regards the body not as an instrument that perceives the world but as the very medium through which the world comes into being. To “lend one’s body to the world” is to move beyond observation, allowing perception itself to become a site of co-creation. The scorched land did not merely appear before me—it moved within me, revealing trauma as both wound and opening.
